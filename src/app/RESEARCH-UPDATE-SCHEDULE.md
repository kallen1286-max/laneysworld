## 📅 Monthly Update Process (30 Minutes)

### Step 1: Visit Each Source (15 min)
1. Open all 4 main sources in separate tabs
2. Scan for news dated within last 30-60 days
3. Copy relevant headlines and descriptions
4. Save URLs for any major announcements

### Step 2: Update Data File (10 min)
1. Open `/data/research-articles.ts`
2. Update article titles, dates, descriptions with latest news
3. Keep NBIA Medical Information Hub as first card (spotlight)
4. Maintain 7-8 total articles
5. Update `lastUpdated` timestamp to current date

### Step 3: Verify & Deploy (5 min)
1. Check that all URLs work
2. Preview site locally to verify cards display correctly
3. Deploy updated site
4. Update this file's "Last Updated" and "Next Update Due" dates
